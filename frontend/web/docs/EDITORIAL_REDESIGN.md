# Editorial redesign

Home opening, workbench and robot retained. New bounded overrides: `assets/editorial.js`, `editorial.css`. Only two imports in `dist/index.html`. `real-photos.js` hue endpoints tightened (60° to 0° to -8°) and simulation brightness reduced to 1.12, original-color toggle stays unfiltered.

## Final experience

- Home remains three sections. Screen two is a photographic notebook: wide autumn branch photograph and a narrower green-fruit study, restrained pointer movement/viewfinder, native image-expansion dialog, and a direct section anchor. Copy no longer overlays the main photograph. Same dark blue-green field transitions into the opening and next screen.
- Screen three retains continuous slider, four snap stages, exact-coordinate 2.25× magnifier and mobile detail expansion. Three photographic sources have distinct uses; one former repeated-variety angle is replaced with a real whole/cut-fruit image. Slider remains an explicit color simulation, not true ripening or inference. Tool row no longer overlaps the lower photograph.
- Roots remains two sections: panoramic canal exhibition with three independently photographed views (decode before swapping, stale-navigation guard, no black frame); followed by a warm editorial page connecting harvest work to project questions via an accessible accordion. Photographs are not represented as team fieldwork.
- About remains two sections: interactive four-step technical pipeline with real project-plan facts and bounded status; archive reading surface with four selectable dossiers and native full-detail dialogs. Original plan figures are reused, not redrawn or fabricated. Validation and independent-test boundaries remain explicit.
